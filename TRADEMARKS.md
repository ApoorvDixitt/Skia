# Trademarks

The Apache License 2.0 covers the **code** in this repository. It deliberately does not grant
rights to names or logos — see [section 6](LICENSE) of the license, which excludes trademarks
from the grant.

So, to be explicit about what that means in practice.

## What you can do

- Use, modify, redistribute, and sell the code, including in closed-source products, under the
  terms of the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
- Fork the project and build something else from it.
- Say truthfully that your work is "based on Skia", "a fork of Skia", or "compatible with Skia".
- Refer to the project by name when discussing, reviewing, or writing about it.

## What needs permission

- Naming your fork or product "Skia", or a name close enough to be confused with it.
- Using the Skia logo as the icon or branding for your own product or service.
- Implying that your fork is the official project, or that it is endorsed by or affiliated with
  the maintainers.

If you would like to do any of these, open an issue and ask — the answer is often yes.

## A note on the name

`Skia` is a provisional codename for this project and collides with
[Google's Skia graphics library](https://skia.org), a separate and much better known project
with which this one has no affiliation. The public-facing name is expected to change before the
first release; see [issue #1](https://github.com/ApoorvDixitt/Skia/issues/1). Until it does,
no claim is made to the term "Skia" beyond this project's own use of it.

## Logo files

The brand assets in [`assets/`](assets) are the project's marks. They are included in the
repository so the app and documentation can render them, and so they can be regenerated — not
as a grant of permission to use them for other purposes.
